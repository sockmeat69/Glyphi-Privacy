# Glyphi-Privacy

Privacy Policy for Glyphi

Effective date: 10 November 2026

Overview

Glyphi is an Android application for creating, customising and exporting icons. Your privacy is important to us.

Information Collection

Glyphi does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information.

The app does not use analytics, advertising, tracking technologies or user accounts.

Images and Files

All image editing and processing takes place locally on your device.

Images imported into Glyphi are not uploaded to external servers or shared with third parties.

Exported icons are saved to the location you choose on your device.

Data Storage and Security

Glyphi does not operate external servers to store your personal information, imported images or exported icons.

Files you create or export remain under your control on your device.

Children's Privacy

Glyphi does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated if Glyphi's features or privacy practices change. Any changes will be published here with an updated effective date.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the developer using the contact information provided on Glyphi's Google Play Store listing.
